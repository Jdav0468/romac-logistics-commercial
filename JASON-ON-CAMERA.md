# Ro-Mac Logistics — Jason Davis On-Camera Scripts
**Talent:** Jason Davis, President  
**Tone:** Straight talk. Dry humor. No movie-trailer voice. Talk like you are on a dock, not in a boardroom.  
**Wardrobe:** Button-down or clean company polo. No tie unless you want one. If we shoot on a dock, safety vest optional — only if it looks natural.  
**Look:** Eye line just off lens is fine. A small smile is earned, not pasted on.  
**Legal:** Do not say we stop all fraud or theft. Say what we do: we vet, we track, we only hire the right carriers.

How to read this: words in CAPS in the stage directions are not spoken. Ellipses are breaths, not drama.

## Direction for Jason

- Talk at about 145–155 words per minute. The :30 hero is ~118 words. You have room to breathe.
- Do not punch "fraud" and "theft" like a news anchor. Say them the way you would say "ice on I-70." Matter of fact.
- The laugh line is "It's a dare." Let it land. Don't wink twice.
- "If the carrier isn't right, we don't book it" is the close of the thought. Pause after it.
- If you stumble, keep the take. A small restart sounds more like you than a perfect read.
- Hands: one gesture on "we don't hand your freight to just anyone." Then stop.

## B-roll under Jason (if we cut away)

- Clean tractor backing to a dock on the first try
- Hands on a rate confirmation / insurance cert (no private customer data)

## What not to say on camera

- Any number we cannot source in the same breath (skip the $725 million line unless legal wants a graphic, not Jason saying it)
- "We guarantee your freight will never be stolen"
- "Every other broker is crooked"
- How a double-broker or theft ring actually works
