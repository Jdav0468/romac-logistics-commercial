## Why this spot exists

American freight in 2025–2026 is not a "find a cheap truck" problem. It is a trust problem.

- Cargo theft losses in the U.S. and Canada hit an estimated **$725 million in 2025**, up about **60%** from 2024. Confirmed incidents rose **18%**. Average loss per theft jumped to about **$274,000**.
- Strategic / deceptive theft (fake pickups, impersonation, double brokering) has exploded — industry reporting puts the rise since 2021 in the **1,500%** range.
- Double brokering alone is widely estimated in the **$700 million to $1 billion** range in annual harm to carriers, with the FBI and industry groups also citing roughly **$800 million**.
- The FBI has warned that cyber-enabled cargo theft is surging: phishing, spoofed broker sites, hijacked load-board identities, and rerouted freight.
- On-highway U.S. diesel was about **$6.53/gallon** the week of September 21, 2026 — more than **$2.70 above** a year earlier. Fuel does not care about your margin.

Ro-Mac's actual offer, from the company site and team:

- Freight brokerage focused on the contiguous United States
- Heavily vetted carrier network: safety, insurance, reliability
- Find the truck, negotiate the rate, manage the move
- Proactive tracking from dock to dock
- Founded 1991. MC# 273349. Licensed, bonded, insured
- President Jason Davis came up as a driver, then operations — he sees the load from the shipper, the carrier, and the driver
- Founder Jennifer Rowe: "My word is my bond."

The joke in the commercial is never "theft is hilarious." The joke is the **absurdity of pretending a load-board stranger is a logistics strategy.** Ro-Mac is the adult in the room who still has a pulse.

4. **They know the street.** Leadership that has driven, dispatched, and told customers the truth.

6. **They are honest about the market.** High fuel is real. Fraud is real. The answer is not the lowest number on a board. The answer is a carrier who will still exist at delivery.

## Production notes

- Keep the three "Not That Guys" cartoonish so nobody watching thinks we are accusing a real carrier type.
- Do **not** show how a theft or double-broker scheme is executed. Cut on the joke, not the method.
- Color: daylight, high-key, dock orange + Ro-Mac navy/white if brand colors exist; otherwise clean industrial with one punchy accent.
- Music: 118–124 BPM, bright bass, no country cliché unless you want a wink on the last beat.
- Legal review: no claim of "zero theft" or "we stop all fraud." Claim what you do: vet, track, stay on it, hire only the right carriers.
- Offer on screen: Book Now at ro-maclogistics.com and (816) 505-4405.
